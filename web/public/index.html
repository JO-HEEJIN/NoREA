NoREA

Put a few fictional records (canaries) into your own public data and sign the evidence with ML-DSA-65. Whoever copies the data copies the canaries too, so you can find them in a suspicious copy and prove it. This tool does not stop copying. It just makes denying it awkward.

Everything runs in this browser. Your data, canaries, and keys are never sent to a server.
